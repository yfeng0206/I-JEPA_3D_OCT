fix(masking): normalise mask budget by anatomy, not raw cells

Raw cell counts flatter whichever arm spends its budget on background, and
they led to a wrong conclusion that the ep30 arms were confounded.

Decomposing the budget over 1,000 slices through the production collator:

  arm               total  anatomy  background  anat-ctx  %%retina-hidden
  envelope_default  117.5     36.2   81.3 (69%%)       6.0          77.3%%
  anatomy            54.3     39.3   15.0 (28%%)       5.7          84.5%%

Envelope spends 69%% of its masking budget on black background. On the axis
that matters the arms are closely matched, and where they differ it is against
us: anatomy hides 8.6%% MORE retina, leaves 5%% LESS retinal context, and covers
a larger fraction of the retina.

Tissue-context per tissue-cell predicted is 0.145 (anatomy) vs 0.166
(envelope) -- our task is marginally harder, not 4.3x easier. The earlier
ratio counted background tokens, which are trivially predictable.

This strengthens the +0.0054 result rather than confounding it, and shows
envelope_matched is NOT a valid control: matching total cells leaves it hiding
only 48%% of the retina with 3.2x more tissue context.

Real finding: to hide comparable retina, rectangles waste 5.4x more budget on
background (81.3 vs 15.0 cells). That is what the anatomy shape buys.
