feat(adapter): placement ablation - encoder tap dominates H0

Tests whether the adapter's segmentation damage comes from the TAP POINT or
from an irreconcilable objective mismatch between MultiMAE MIRAGE and I-JEPA.

Three taps, everything else fixed (same teacher, images, steps, trunk, lr):
  enc  pre proj_dec        (256,768)  whole 95.5M decoder downstream
  mid  pre ConvNeXt blocks (384,64,64)
  h0   pre final_layer     (384,64,64)  CURRENT - 1,540 params downstream

alpha is swept because the same alpha yields very different transfer at
different taps, making matched-alpha comparison meaningless.

Cost to achieve MATCHED L_rel transfer:
  ~25%%  enc a=.05 -0.0004 (p=0.14 neutral) | h0 a=.50 -0.0048 (p=3e-4 harm)
  ~46%%  enc a=.10 -0.0011              | h0 a=1.0 -0.0162
  ~76%%  mid a=.05 -0.0021              | h0 cannot reach

h0 is dominated everywhere: it saturates at 29.9%% transfer for alpha<=0.5.
Efficiency (L_rel per unit Dice damage): enc 70,000 vs h0 6,268 -> 11x.

The stated risk that a frozen decoder would AMPLIFY an off-manifold
perturbation does not materialise. Amplification (|dH0|/|H0|)/(|dZ|/|Z|):
  h0 1.00 (by construction, validates the metric) | mid 0.44-0.57 | enc 0.39-0.49
The decoder roughly halves the relative perturbation.

Why h0 transfers so little: Gram agreement with JEPA before training is
enc 0.313, mid 0.371, h0 0.062 -- H0 is a near-logit representation whose
similarity structure is essentially uncorrelated with JEPA's.

Harness validated: no-adapter path reproduces frozen Dice 0.9457 exactly and
the h0 tap returns amplification 1.00 as it must.

Caveat recorded: at matched MASK RELOCATION rather than matched transfer, h0
is cheapest. This settles placement, not whether the adapter earns its place.
