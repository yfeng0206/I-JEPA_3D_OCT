feat(adapter): class-conditioned structural loss + separation barrier

Implements transfer-strengths/preserve-strengths objective: I-JEPA teaches
only safe blocks {I-I, C-C, I-B, C-B}, the I-C block it gets wrong is excluded,
both sides z-scored per image, and a one-sided barrier
L_sep = relu(delta_frozen - delta_adapted)^2 protects MIRAGE's separation.
Pseudo-classes come from frozen MIRAGE itself - no external labels.

PREREQUISITE FINDING: every prior adapter experiment trained on slice 100 ONLY
(one depth of 200). A stratified cache spanning 100 depths is strictly better
(cross-depth generalisation 21.84%% vs 21.18%%, Dice +0.0001 vs -0.00035).
Saturation is real regardless: 1200/2400/4800 -> 20.1/23.2/24.3%%, so one pass
over 4800 stratified slices is the right budget.

RESULT 1 - the barrier works exactly as designed. At alpha=0.50 Gram-MSE
destroys 61%% of the inner/choroid separation (+0.3637 -> +0.1434); structural
preserves 104%% (+0.3785). lambda=0 ablation confirms the barrier is the active
ingredient.

RESULT 2 - it does NOT remove the Dice damage, refuting the premise that
separation collapse CAUSES it:
  alpha   Gram-MSE   structural
  0.05    +0.00010   +0.00028
  0.10    -0.00072   -0.00062
  0.25    -0.00324   -0.00970
  0.50    -0.00609   -0.02042
At alpha=0.50 structural holds separation ABOVE frozen and still loses 0.0204
Dice. Class geometry and segmentation damage are INDEPENDENT axes: the barrier
controls the first, alpha (drift magnitude) controls the second.

Honest positive: at matched (neutral) Dice, structural preserves 97%% of the
separation vs Gram-MSE's 84%% -- 17%% better. Use structural lam=100, alpha=0.10.

RESULT 3 - teacher maturity dominates. An early (ep27) teacher is 3.8x more
damaging than ep100 under Gram-MSE (-0.0229 vs -0.0061). Bears directly on
refresh cadence: adapting against an immature JEPA is expensive.

RESULT 4 - the change is not diffuse noise. It concentrates at the deep
choroid boundary and is almost entirely GAINED cells: the adapter
systematically extends the retina downward. GOALS says that is wrong.

Also fixes a real bug: saved adapter filenames omitted alpha, so an alpha
sweep silently overwrote earlier checkpoints.
