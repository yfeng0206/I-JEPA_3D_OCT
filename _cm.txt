fix: encode full identity in adapter filenames

Last instance of the incomplete-identity bug class. Filenames carried only
alpha, so runs differing in epochs, lr, training size, depth or width silently
overwrote each other -- and other scripts then consumed whichever file
happened to survive.

adapter_structural_loss:  adapter_{teacher}_{loss}_a{NNN}_e{E}_lr{LR}_n{N}.pt
adapter_placement_ablation: adapter_{tap}_a{NNN}_d{D}_w{W}_lr{LR}_n{N}.pt
Both also store lr and n_train inside the checkpoint.

Consumers (class_relation_probe, cadence_guide_diff, fairvision_before_after)
now accept a unique prefix and fall back to a glob, so they keep working with
both old and new names and fail loudly when nothing matches rather than
silently producing a figure with no adapters in it.

117 tests pass.
