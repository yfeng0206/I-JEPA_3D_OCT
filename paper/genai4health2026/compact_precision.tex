\section{Probe precision control}
\label{app:fp32}

The original rectangle-policy probes use mixed precision, while the
anatomy-shaped and coverage-policy probes use fp32. All primary differences in
Table~\ref{tab:main} use a baseline matched in both epoch and probe precision.
Probe precision is distinct from pretraining target-encoder precision.
We repeated the recorded mixed-precision probes with autocast disabled in
feature extraction, head fitting and evaluation. Each comparison uses the
same frozen encoder. The recorded changes are smaller than the principal
policy differences.
